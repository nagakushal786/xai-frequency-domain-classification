\section{Introduction}
\begin{frame}{Introduction}
  \begin{itemize}
      \item Traditional CNNs work well with spatial data but have trouble handling high-resolution and large images. Using frequency domain processing with FFT (Fast Fourier Transform) makes CNN training and use faster and more efficient in terms of memory.
      \item Explainable AI techniques, like Grad-CAM, are commonly used to understand how models make predictions. They create visual explanations that show which parts of an image are most important for the model's decision.
      \item A major research question is whether XAI methods, such as Grad-CAM, can be successfully used with CNNs that operate in the frequency domain?
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\section{Research Gaps Identified}
\begin{frame}{Research Gaps Identified}
  \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Absence of Grad-CAM (or any XAI) for CNNs Trained on Frequency Domain Inputs}: None explained how to interpret or explain the predictions made by these frequency-domain-trained CNNs using XAI methods like Grad-CAM.
      \item \textbf{Lack of Mapping Explanations from Frequency Domain Features to Original Spatial Images}: No existing research has looked into using Grad-CAM on frequency-domain feature maps from CNNs that were trained using frequency-domain data. The main issue is figuring out how to convert these frequency-based explanations back into the original spatial images to find which pixels contributed to the model's decisions.
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\section{Research Gaps Identified 1}
\begin{frame}{Research Gaps Identified}
  \begin{itemize}
      \item \textbf{Unexplored Interpretability Challenges for Frequency-Domain Architectures}: The interpretability of models that operate primarily or entirely in the frequency domain is not addressed. How do specific frequency components, identified as "important" by an XAI method, translate to human-understandable visual features in the original image?
  \end{itemize}
\end{frame}

\section{Problem Statement}
\begin{frame}{Problem Statement}
  \textit{\textbf{How can the predictions made by a CNN that was trained and tested using images in the frequency domain be clearly explained in terms of the specific areas in the original spatial images, using Grad-CAM and explainable AI techniques?}}
\end{frame}

\section{Objectives}
\begin{frame}{Objectives}
  \begin{enumerate}
      \item Adapting Grad-CAM for Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) Operating in the Frequency Domain.
      \item Creating a strong approach to connect frequency-domain Grad-CAM explanations back to the original image in the spatial domain.
      \item Evaluating how clear and accurate the generated spatial explanations are?, both in terms of the amount of information provided and the quality of that information.
      \item Studying how specific aspects in the frequency domain affect the ability to understand spatial information?
  \end{enumerate}
\end{frame}

\section{Proposed Methodology}
\begin{frame}{Proposed Methodology}
  \begin{enumerate}
      \item Establishing the Frequency-Domain CNN pipeline
      \begin{enumerate}
          \item Image data pre-processing for Frequency Domain Input.
          \item Designing a CNN Architecture for Frequency Domain Inputs.
      \end{enumerate}
      \item Adapting Grad-CAM for Frequency-Domain CNNs
      \begin{enumerate}
          \item Selecting the Target Convolutional Layer.
          \item Computing Class-Specific Gradients in the Frequency Domain.
          \item Computing Neuron Importance Weights in the Frequency Domain.
          \item Generating Raw Frequency-Domain Grad-CAM Map.
      \end{enumerate}
      \item Mapping Explanations to the Spatial Domain
      \begin{enumerate}
          \item Inverse Transform the Frequency-Domain Grad-CAM Map.
          \item Upsample and Refine the Spatial Explanation Map.
      \end{enumerate}
      \item Evaluation
  \end{enumerate}
\end{frame}

\section{Conclusion}
\begin{frame}{Conclusion}
  \textit{This project effectively connects the efficiency of frequency domain CNN classification with transparent AI using Grad-CAM. This work improves both the speed and reliability of deep learning systems.}
\end{frame}
